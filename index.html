About Me

I am Bowen Zhang, a Lecturer and Master's Supervisor at the School of Economics and Management, Beijing University of Chemical Technology.

My research focuses on data analytics and optimization, smart urban transportation management, and facility location theory, methods and applications. My work covers bike-sharing operations and rebalancing, public transportation optimization, electric vehicle battery swapping station location, and network optimization under uncertainty.

I received my Ph.D. in Management from Beijing University of Chemical Technology in 2023. I undertook government-funded joint doctoral training in Statistics and Operations Research at the University of Lisbon, and conducted postdoctoral research at the School of Information Science and Technology, Beijing University of Chemical Technology, from 2023 to 2025.

I welcome inquiries from prospective graduate students interested in data analytics, operations research and optimization, smart transportation, and intelligent optimization algorithms. A foundation in mathematics and programming, an interest in exploring research problems, and a willingness to develop academic reading and writing skills in English are encouraged. Get in touch →
